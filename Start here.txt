ZERO EQUALIZER 1.2

Extract the full ZIP and open ZeroEqualizer.exe. The app uses Windows 10 or 11
and the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime. You can preview controls and save presets before
connecting audio.

Connect Windows audio

1. Click Audio setup in Zero Equalizer and choose Install Equalizer APO.
   Zero Equalizer verifies the official installer before opening it. If the
   download does not complete, open Project page, download Equalizer APO 1.4.2,
   then select Use downloaded installer.
2. In its Device Selector, enable Equalizer APO on the playback device you
   actually listen through. Enable its post-mix effect and restart Windows if
   prompted. Keep device audio enhancements enabled.
3. Open Zero Equalizer, click Refresh, select that playback output, and click
   Connect EQ. Play music or a video, try Bass Boost, and compare with Bypass.

The Equalizer APO installer allows unsigned Audio Processing Objects in the
Windows audio engine. Equalizer APO documents this setting and its effect on
protected audio here:
https://sourceforge.net/p/equalizerapo/wiki/Developer%20documentation/

Using the app

- Bass and Treble are broad shelf controls. The other ten sliders adjust the
  frequency bands individually. Double-click a slider to reset it.
- The graph shows the expected curve at 48 kHz, including the manual preamp.
  Preamp defaults to 0 dB. Adjust it yourself to change the overall EQ level.
  Presets and Reset EQ keep the preamp value. The 0 dB button resets it.
- Pick a built-in preset or save your own. Each output remembers its own curve
  and bypass setting. Move EQ here switches the connected output explicitly.
- Bypass temporarily removes Zero Equalizer's filters and keeps your settings.
  Disconnect EQ removes its configuration block. Closing the app keeps the last
  applied settings active. On first connect, the app saves the original config
  alongside config.txt as config.txt.zero-equalizer-backup.
- EQ applies to apps using shared Windows audio on the connected output. ASIO
  and WASAPI exclusive playback can bypass Windows audio effects.
